Research

I am seeking a PhD position in radiomics and radiogenomics — the quantitative analysis of medical images and their integration with genomic data to discover imaging biomarkers, predict treatment response, and personalise cancer care. My MSc dissertation on medical image concept detection gave me a foundation in radiology image pipelines and multi-label deep learning. I want to build on this toward imaging-genomics fusion models: extracting radiomic features from tumour images and linking them to gene expression, mutation profiles, or drug response data to find clinically actionable patterns in precision oncology.

MSc Dissertation · 2023

Concept Detection in Medical Images

Built a ResNet18 multi-label classifier on radiology images with UMLS/CUI concept labels (ImageCLEF/ROCO-style data). Showed that accuracy alone misleads clinical evaluation — grounding in this work motivates my interest in quantitative radiomic feature extraction and meaningful biomarker validation.

Active · 2026

MONAI Medical Imaging Baseline

Implemented a clean medical image classification pipeline (MONAI + PyTorch) on MedNIST — 0.9986 validation accuracy — with full evaluation including confusion matrix, precision, recall, and F1. Establishing foundational skills toward tumour image analysis and radiomic feature pipelines.

Building · 2026

Oncology Imaging & Radiomics Workspace

Developing a reproducible research workspace for oncology imaging concept detection — DICOM preprocessing, PyRadiomics feature extraction, and structured experiment tracking — as a foundation for radiogenomics integration work.

Research Interest

Imaging-Genomics Fusion for Cancer

Investigating multi-modal fusion approaches that link radiomic imaging features with genomic data (gene expression, somatic mutations) to predict drug response, tumour subtype, and patient outcomes — the core of the radiogenomics PhD direction I am pursuing.
