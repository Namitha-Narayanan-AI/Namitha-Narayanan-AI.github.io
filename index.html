Research

Research focus

My research focuses on clinically grounded machine learning for cancer imaging, particularly how imaging representations, lesion characteristics, and rigorous model evaluation can contribute to malignancy assessment and earlier cancer detection.

My work has progressed from multi-label concept detection in medical images to reproducible 2D clinical-imaging experiments and, more recently, volumetric thoracic CT using LIDC-IDRI. I currently work with raw DICOM data, CT volume reconstruction, Hounsfield-unit conversion, radiologist annotations, spacing-aware 3D lesion extraction, and deep-learning models for lesion-level malignancy-risk assessment.

I am particularly interested in quantitative cancer imaging, multimodal representations, radiomics and radiogenomics, explainable clinical AI, and machine-learning methods that remain robust and clinically meaningful beyond aggregate predictive performance.

Oncology Imaging Concept Detection

Independent research · 2026 — present
Active research

A reproducible medical-imaging research project investigating clinically meaningful evaluation and malignancy-risk modelling across two-dimensional and three-dimensional imaging settings.

Current study — 3D CT lesion modelling

The current study uses the LIDC-IDRI thoracic CT dataset. The pipeline begins with raw DICOM series and performs geometric slice ordering, Hounsfield-unit conversion, radiologist XML annotation parsing, exact ROI-to-slice alignment, spacing-aware 3D lesion extraction, patient-level data separation, and lesion-level deep-learning analysis.

Current stage: The end-to-end 3D CT pipeline has been completed through PyTorch-based malignancy-risk modelling, validation-based threshold selection, held-out test evaluation, ROC/PR analysis, probability analysis, and structured error analysis. A manuscript based on this work is currently in preparation.
Methodological foundation — 2D clinical baseline

An earlier chest-radiograph experiment established the project's clinical evaluation framework using sensitivity, specificity, balanced accuracy, threshold analysis, false-positive and false-negative investigation, and Grad-CAM. The study highlighted substantial sensitivity-specificity imbalance and possible shortcut behaviour, motivating a transition toward richer volumetric lesion modelling.

Investigating Concept Detection Techniques in Medical Images

MSc Dissertation · Heriot-Watt University · 2023
Completed

Investigated multi-label concept detection using approximately 75,000–80,000 medical and radiology images with associated captions and UMLS/CUI-style clinical concept labels. Developed reproducible preprocessing, training, validation, and evaluation pipelines using PyTorch, PyTorch Lightning, and ResNet18.

Research focus: Analysed sparse supervision, class imbalance, long-tail clinical concepts, image-text relationships, and the limitations of aggregate accuracy in medical-image understanding.
